# Compare two groups
Compare two groups following this recipe:
1) Inspect the grouping variable and the outcome.
2) Check normality (shapiro.test) and equality of variance.
3) Choose the right test: t.test for normal, wilcox.test otherwise; chi-square/Fisher for categorical.
4) Report effect size / test statistic, p-value, and a boxplot.
5) State the clinical meaning and assumptions.
